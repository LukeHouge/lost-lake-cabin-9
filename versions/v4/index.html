Two lakes, one front door

Big-lake adventure, or a quiet little lake of your own

Gull Lake

About 10,000 acres · part of the Gull Lake chain

  • A public boat launch right across the road, next to Zorbaz. Easy to bring your own boat.
  • Resort docks for guests' boats, a short walk down the stairs, plus a spot to park your trailer
  • Or rent from Lost Lake Lodge: pontoons, fishing boats, and a ski boat for tubing, waterskiing and wakeboarding
  • Boat to Zorbaz, Bar Harbor, Ernie's and Dock 77, or through the chain to downtown Nisswa
  • The Gull Point sandbar, and walleye, bass and pike
  • Ice fishing and snowmobile trails in winter

Lost Lake

Private, spring-fed, and separate from the main lake

  • A soft sand beach with a slide and a big floating raft
  • Lounge chairs and fire pits for bonfires every night
  • Playground, volleyball and lawn games
  • Free kayaks, canoes and paddle boards
  • Wooded walking trails all the way around

Lost Lake Lodge

All the resort perks, a short walk from the door

Cottage 9 sits within Lost Lake Lodge, a small, quiet resort that has welcomed families to Gull Lake since 1946. It covers 80 acres of woods and water, about five minutes from downtown Nisswa.

Restaurant & bar

Lake-view dinners with wine pairings, happy hour, live music nights and Sunday brunch.

Bike from the door

The paved Gull Lake Trail loops the lake and links through Nisswa to the 120-mile Paul Bunyan State Trail. From the cabin it's almost all trail, with one short stretch on County Road 77 until the last section is finished.

Sleds, skis & the ice

The Gull Lake Drifters groom about 120 miles of snowmobile trail around Nisswa and up the Paul Bunyan corridor. Gull Lake hosts one of the biggest ice-fishing contests in the world, and Mount Ski Gull and miles of cross-country trails are close by.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I book Cottage 9?

Book online through Lost Lake Lodge or call (218) 963-2681. Cottage 9 is one of the resort's 4-bedroom cottages, so ask for Cottage 9 by name. Tell them if you want the whole house, the 3-bedroom main house, or the lower suite.

How new is the cabin?

Brand new. Cottage 9 was built from fall 2022 to spring 2023 and welcomed its first guests in July 2023. Everything from the kitchen to the bedrooms is new construction.

How many people does it sleep?

Up to 10 guests. There's a king on the main level, two queens in each of the two upstairs bedrooms, a king on the lower level, and a queen sleeper sofa in the loft.

Check-in, check-out and minimum stay?

Check-in is at 4 pm and check-out is at 10 am. There's a 2-night minimum.

Can we bring our own boat?

Yes. There's a public boat launch right across the road from the resort, next to Zorbaz, plus other public launches around Gull Lake. The resort has docks where guests can keep their boats, and a place to store your trailer. No boat? Lost Lake Lodge rents pontoons, fishing boats and a ski boat, and kayaks, canoes and paddle boards are free.

Is there shopping and EV charging nearby?

Yes. Nisswa (5 minutes) has groceries, shops and restaurants. Brainerd–Baxter (about 20 minutes) has Costco, Target, Best Buy, Home Depot, PetSmart and more, plus a Tesla Supercharger and other DC fast chargers.

Are pets allowed?

No. Cottage 9 is pet-free, which is good news for guests with allergies.

What fees and deposits apply?

A 50% deposit is due when you book, and the balance at check-in. The 7.375% Minnesota sales tax and the 8% resort fee are added to the nightly rate.

What's the cancellation policy?

For summer stays, the deposit is refunded if you cancel more than 60 days before arrival, minus a nonrefundable 8% fee. In other seasons, cancel at least 14 days out. The resort's terms at booking are what apply.

Is the restaurant open year-round?

Restaurant hours vary by season, so check with the lodge before an off-season trip. Cottage 9's full kitchen means you're covered either way.
