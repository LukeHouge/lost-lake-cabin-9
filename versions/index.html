Cottage 9 website: pick a look

Each card opens a full version of the site. The newest version (v3) adds motion: a sunrise intro, a lake map that draws itself, a day-to-dusk timeline and a clickable floor explorer. Its theme can switch with the season automatically, or stay on one look all year. Pick a favorite, or note the pieces you like from each.

v3 · Summer

Lake Life

Sunset over the docks with coral and deep-lake blue. Boating, wake surfing and bonfires.
v3 · Fall

Gold & Ember

Aerial fall color with burnt orange and bark brown. Color drives, grouse woods and fire pits.
v3 · Winter

Blue Hour

Icy blues, warm amber buttons and falling snow. Ice fishing, sleds and skis. Still needs real winter photos.
v3 · Accents off

Clean

The same as v3, without the little hand-drawn icons (loons, oars, skis, bonfires) in the ticker, perk cards, timeline and logbook. Compare with any v3 card above.
v2 · Static

No Animations

The same seasonal design without the motion: no intro, no drawing map, and room cards instead of the floor explorer.
v1 · Original

First Draft

The first simple version, with an illustrated sunset and no photos yet. Kept for comparison.

The live site picks its theme from the date: summer in May–August, fall in September–October, winter in November–April. Use the "Theme" pill in the corner of any v2 or v3 page to flip between looks. On v3, the "Accents" button turns the hand-drawn icons on and off.
