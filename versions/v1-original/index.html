Lost Lake Lodge

A small, quiet resort on Gull Lake since 1946

Lost Lake Lodge covers 80 acres of woods and water between the Gull Lake Narrows and its own private, spring-fed Lost Lake. It's a quiet, old-school Up North resort, and downtown Nisswa is about five minutes away.

  • Two lakes. Boat the Gull Lake chain, or paddle and fish the quiet private lake.
  • Dinner is a short walk away. The on-site restaurant has served dinner since 1946. It also has Sunday brunch and live music.
  • Boats and toys. You can rent pontoons, fishing boats and a ski boat. Kayaks, canoes and paddle boards are free.
  • The beach. A private sandy beach with docks, a slide and a big floating raft.
  • The Recovery Room. A private swim spa, sauna and workout space you can reserve.
  • Trails and games. Wooded trails around Lost Lake, plus volleyball, basketball and shuffleboard.

Seasons & rates

A good trip in every season

Summer is classic lake life. Fall and winter are quieter, cozier, and a lot cheaper.

Summer on Gull Lake

Boat to Zorbaz or Bar Harbor for dinner, spend the afternoon at the sandbar, and catch the Wednesday turtle races in Nisswa. At night it's s'mores and pool downstairs.

  • Boating, tubing and swimming off the beach raft
  • Walleye, bass and panfish on the Gull Lake chain
  • Golf at The Classic, Grand View and Deacon's Lodge
  • Paul Bunyan Trail and Cuyuna mountain biking
Whole house from$1,025per night

Fall color & grouse woods

The colors usually peak from late September to early October. The lake goes quiet, the golf courses get easy to book, and the bonfires come back.

  • Fall-color drives and hikes on the Lost Lake trails
  • Ruffed grouse, woodcock and deer on nearby public land
  • Shoulder-season golf and fall fishing
  • Breweries in Nisswa, Baxter and Pequot Lakes
Whole house from$650per night

Ice, sleds & a warm cabin

Gull Lake hosts the Brainerd Jaycees Ice Fishing Extravaganza, one of the biggest ice-fishing contests in the world. The area also has more than 1,000 miles of snowmobile trails. Come back to a warm house and the pool table.

  • Ice fishing on Gull Lake
  • Snowmobiling on the area's trail network
  • Cross-country skiing, snowshoeing and fat biking
  • Downhill skiing at Mount Ski Gull
Whole house from$425per night

Good things nearby

Boat-up bars & supper clubs

Zorbaz on Gull Lake is just down Lost Lake Road. Bar Harbor Supper Club, built over the water in 1937, Ernie's on Gull and Dock 77 are all classic stops by boat.

Downtown Nisswa

About five minutes away, with the Wednesday turtle races (a summer tradition since 1963), the Chocolate Ox, Turtle Town Books, and breweries.

Golf

Three courses within six miles, plus The Classic at Madden's, Grand View's Pines and Preserve, and Deacon's Lodge in Breezy Point.

Trails

The Paul Bunyan State Trail runs about 120 miles through Nisswa. The Cuyuna mountain-bike trails are a short drive away.

On the water

The Gull Lake chain covers about 13,000 acres. You'll find walleye, pike and bass, the Gull Point sandbar, and the 1912 Gull Lake Dam recreation area.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book Cottage 9?

Book through Lost Lake Lodge online or by calling (218) 963-2681. Cottage 9 is one of the resort's 4-bedroom cottages, so ask for Cottage 9 by name. Let them know if you want the whole house, the 3-bedroom, or the lower suite.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 4 pm and check-out is at 10 am. There's a 2-night minimum.

Are pets allowed?

No. Cottage 9 is pet-free, which is good news for guests with allergies. Some other cottages at the resort do allow pets.

What fees and deposits should I expect?

A 50% deposit is due when you book, and the balance at check-in. The 7.375% Minnesota sales tax and the 8% resort fee are added to the nightly rate.

What's the cancellation policy?

For summer stays, the deposit is refunded if you cancel more than 60 days before arrival, minus a nonrefundable 8% fee. In other seasons, cancel at least 14 days out to keep your deposit. The resort's terms at booking are what apply.

Is the restaurant open year-round?

The restaurant's hours vary by season, so check with the resort before an off-season stay. The full kitchen in Cottage 9 means you're covered either way.
