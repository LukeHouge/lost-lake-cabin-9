# Photo shot list

Drop originals (full-res JPG/HEIC) in this folder. I'll resize and compress them (aim: under ~300 KB each, 2000px wide for the hero).

Highest impact first:
1. **Hero:** best lake view, ideally at sunset or golden hour, horizontal (`hero.jpg`)
2. Deck/porch looking at the water
3. Kitchen (wide, lights on, counters clear)
4. Great room with TV/fireplace
5. Screened porch set up (drinks, cards, blankets)
6. Loft and its view out the window
7. Game room: pool table + wet bar
8. Each bedroom: king suite, both queen rooms, lower king
9. Bathrooms (one or two good ones)
10. Resort: beach + raft, docks, Lost Lake trail, restaurant, boats
11. Seasonal: fall color, winter/ice, summer boating
12. People having fun (with permission): fishing, boating, game night

Tips: shoot in daylight with lights on, horizontal, from doorway corners at chest height. Phone photos are fine.
